About the Festival

A Large-Scale Music Festival

UBFest is a major multi-genre music festival held at Center Stadium, bringing together pop, rock, metal and hip-hop in one unforgettable event. Six headline acts — Olivia Rodrigo, Metallica, Linkin Park, 21 Pilots, Kendrick Lamar and Travis Scott — perform for tens of thousands of fans.

Like Other Large Festivals

Large music festivals such as Coachella, Glastonbury and Lollapalooza draw crowds of 100,000 or more, run for several days across multiple stages, and feature art installations, food vendors, camping areas and brand activations. They are major economic events, creating thousands of temporary jobs and generating millions of dollars for host cities.
